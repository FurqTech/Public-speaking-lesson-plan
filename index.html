What is debating?

Debating is basically a fight about a specific topic. you must have seen this kind of thing over the news where different people including the news reporter are talking about a specific topic. Yes, that is also debating. There's an example of debate. Suppose you are talking about the topic smartphones should be allowed in school. Now you'll have to decide that are you going against it or are you going for it. And if another person chooses opposite to yours than you'll have to defend your opinion using reasons.

The way to debate

The way to debate must seem simple but it won't be simple. So if you're in a debate conversation already then you'll need to defend your opinion but if you want to start a whole new debate conversation then you'll need to follow the given structure without saying any "Uh" or "Um". And you'll need to have full confidence and flow to talk in you. If you talk with flow, then you won't be interrupted by "Uh" or "Umm". So the structure to do a debate conversation is given in the next page.

Page 4/4

Structure of debate

So before starting your debate conversation, You'll need to start with:

1: Greetings

You'll need to greet someone you want to talk to about a specific topic.

2: Introduction

Give your introduction after greeting that person

3: Topic and opinion

Tell about your topic and after that tell your opinion about it.

4: Reason

Then you'll need to tell the reason for you having that specific opinion to defend your opinion.

5: Summary

You'll also need to give a small summary so that people can understand that why do you have that specific opinion, meaning that you'll need to tell a small anecdote of your life It's your choice. If you have an anecdote related to your opinion, you can summarize it.

6: Thank the person you are talking to.

You'll have to thank the person you were having a debate conversation with.

7: Stop

That's it. You can stop at the end of the conversation
